## Distortion correction
method: reversing phase-encode polarity (Holland et al., 2010); displacement field
  from separate spin-echo calibration scans, adjusted for between-scan motion, applied
  to the gradient-echo series; then gradient-nonlinearity correction (Jovicich 2006).
  sentence: "B0 distortions are corrected using the same reversing polarity method used
  for the dMRI (Holland et al., 2010)." ; "Images are next corrected for distortions
  due to gradient nonlinearities (Jovicich et al., 2006)."
  variants: gradient-nonlinearity transforms are scanner-specific, "provided by MRI
  scanner manufacturers" (sMRI section).

## Stated by Hagler, no field above
- Motion: each frame registered to the first; between-scan alignment to the scan
  nearest the middle, rigid, cubic interpolation.
- Native space, 2.4 mm; surface sampling 1 mm into gray matter, nearest neighbour.
- Initial frames removed: "removal of initial frames4" — the count is in footnote 4,
  not in this excerpt; likely scanner-specific; check the footnote.
- Rest regression: quadratic trends, motion (six parameters plus derivatives and
  squares — 18 or 24 regressors, ambiguous), WM, ventricles, whole brain + first
  derivatives; masks from aseg, eroded one voxel.
- Motion estimates notch-filtered (IIR) at 0.31–0.43 Hz (respiration).
- Frames with FD > 0.3 mm excluded from the regression; band-pass 0.009–0.08 Hz after
  regression.
- Rest censoring: FD > 0.2 mm; runs of < 5 contiguous frames; ROI-SD outliers > 3 MAD.
