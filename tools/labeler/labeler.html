State definitions and standing rulings

Standing rulings

  1. Values as stated, no unit conversion. Write the width as the paper gives it (FWHM, sigma or RMS radius, in its own unit) and filter edges in Hz or seconds as written. Converting a unit is an inference, not a label.
  2. Smoothing of statistical maps is not BOLD smoothing. Smoothing applied to statistical maps or other analysis-stage outputs does not count: label smoothing not_reported and describe it in notes.
  3. COBIDAS-literal filtering. COBIDAS asks whether high- or low-pass filtering was applied and where in the pipeline, and for drift regressors a specified cut-off. No unit is required, so a stated filter with a non-Hz or non-numeric edge is reported, with the value kept as stated. Frequency selection by wavelet decomposition counts as temporal filtering.

Motion correction, method state

named_tool
A specific tool or method is named for this step (e.g., MCFLIRT, SPM realign, AFNI 3dvolreg).
described_only
The operation is stated but no tool is named (e.g., "motion correction was performed"). Performance is established; the tool is not.
deferred
The method is not stated but attributed elsewhere: a citation, or a named pipeline whose motion step the paper does not itself describe. A citation that identifies the method is deferred, not named_tool.
absent
No statement about motion correction at all.
stated_not_performed
The paper explicitly says motion correction was not performed.

Motion correction is EPI to EPI (or to a within-run reference); EPI to anatomical is coregistration. The realignment step is distinct from the motion parameters used as regressors. For named_tool, attribution is step when the tool is named as the instrument of the realignment itself, and package when the paper names exactly one realignment-capable package for its preprocessing or analysis as a whole, names no step-level tool, and asserts the motion step (protocol v1.8). A pipeline or wrapper is not a package.

Smoothing and filtering, state

reported
The step is stated with its parameters.
partially_reported
Some of the step is stated, some not.
not_reported
Nothing about the step.
stated_not_performed
The paper says the step was not done; a stated negative is a report.
deferred
Attributed to a citation or a pipeline.

One row per paper × applies_to. applies_to is the paper's own words for what the value applies to (a derived measure, an analysis, a stream); "all" is the ordinary case. Quotes come from the PDF text layer; if a glyph or a page header broke the text, clean the quote and say what changed in notes.

The remaining COBIDAS rows

Same five states as smoothing and filtering. Each field is named as in the schema and is written as the paper states it, in its own unit: no unit conversion, no mapping to a controlled value. The state carries "performed" (stated_not_performed, or one of the others). Sub-cards (CompCor and ICA denoising under nuisance regression; scrubbing and despiking under volume censoring) are labelled on their own.

  1. A confound that is computed or made available (framewise displacement, DVARS, motion parameters, CompCor components) is not applied unless the paper says it was regressed out.
  2. "Annotated as outliers" is not censoring unless the frames were removed or interpolated.
  3. A step the data provider applied to a released dataset is labelled as applied; name the release in notes.
  4. "Co-registered to correct for head motion" is motion correction, not coregistration.
  5. Dummy volumes discarded by the scanner and dummy volumes discarded in preprocessing both count for T1 stabilization (nonsteadystate_removal); say which in notes.

Four more rows (added 2026-11-11)

These fields are the label's own, not one schema step's; values as stated, like the rest.

Gradient distortion correction
Correction for gradient nonlinearity (scanner gradient distortion). Susceptibility distortion is the Distortion correction card.
Intersubject registration
Each subject to a common template. volume_or_surface: volume, surface or both; template as named (e.g. MNI152NLin2009cAsym, fsaverage5); resolution in its own unit; warp_type: rigid, affine, nonlinear, or the transform as named.
Resting-state fMRI feature
One row per measure computed, applies_to = the measure. band_hz as stated; reho_neighbourhood (e.g. 27 voxels) and reho_measure (e.g. Kendall's W) for ReHo only.
Software
One row per package, keyed by name ("add package"). version_as_stated exactly as written, blank when none; used_for in the paper's words. State per package: reported when a version is stated, partially_reported when named without one, deferred when its version is left to a citation. A paper naming no software: one row, name blank, not_reported.
